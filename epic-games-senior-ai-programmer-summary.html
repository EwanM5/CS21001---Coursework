Senior AI Programmer

Epic Games · Unreal Engine · Engineering

Requisition ID: R27502

Location: Montreal, Canada

Product: Unreal Engine

Role summary

Epic is seeking an experienced programmer for its Unreal Engine non-player-character (NPC) technology team. The team develops character systems used across Unreal Engine, Fortnite, and UEFN, including behaviour, perception, navigation, and in-game generative AI.

Main responsibilities

  • Develop and maintain core NPC behaviour, perception, and navigation systems.
  • Design technical proposals and APIs for developers using Unreal Engine.
  • Explore real-time generative AI features with machine-learning research teams.
  • Improve system performance, memory use, and scalability.

Required experience and skills

  • Experience shipping AAA games and building production game-AI systems, such as behaviour trees, perception, or pathfinding.
  • Professional Unreal Engine experience, ideally including system design.
  • Strong C++ programming skills and experience designing core AI technology.
  • Clear communication and collaborative working skills, with the ability to work independently.

Additional experience that may be useful

  • Connecting large language models or other generative models to real-time software.
  • Using AI coding assistants to support development work.
  • Working on live-service games.

Benefits overview

The advert describes employer-paid benefits for employees and dependants. These include medical, dental, vision, disability, and life cover; a retirement plan with employer matching; an employee assistance programme; and access to therapy and coaching.

About Epic Games

Epic Games develops games and Unreal Engine, which is also used to create interactive experiences and 3D content in other industries. The advert highlights collaboration, innovation, inclusion, and equal-opportunity employment.
